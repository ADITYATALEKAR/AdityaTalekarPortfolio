01 About

Civil engineer turned AI engineer.

I started out running infrastructure projects in Pune, where I spent three years replacing manual reporting with Python and Power BI. That habit of turning tedious work into systems took me to Queen Mary University of London for an MSc in Data Science & AI, which I finished with Distinction.

Since then I've worked on one question: how do you make LLM systems trustworthy enough to run unattended? My answer is usually more engineering, not a bigger model — agents that gather evidence, critics that check it, confidence gates, caches, and a human approval step where it matters. I've shipped that idea as a VS Code extension, a security platform for banks and production pipelines for a research firm.

Today I teach AI to engineering students in Kolhapur, mentor hackathon teams, and take on consulting work.
