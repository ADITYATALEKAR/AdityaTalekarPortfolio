About

I’m an AI engineer who started as a civil engineer. Three years of running infrastructure projects taught me to automate the work that slows everyone down; an MSc in Data Science & AI at Queen Mary University of London (Distinction) turned that habit into a career.

Since then I’ve focused on one question: how do you make LLM systems trustworthy enough to run unattended? My answer is usually more engineering, not a bigger model — agents that gather evidence, critics that check it, and a human approval step where it matters. I’ve shipped that as a VS Code extension used by developers, a security platform for banks, and production pipelines for a research firm.

Now: Assistant Professor of AI at Dr. D. Y. Patil Pratishthan’s College of Engineering, Kolhapur — and open to AI engineering roles and consulting.
